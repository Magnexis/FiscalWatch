GAO source-methodology update · October 26, 2026

The May 2026 GAO-26-108932 open-recommendations model estimates $132 billion–$251 billion in potential future financial benefits across the broad GAO portfolio. This is not an annual figure, not realized savings, not the total of FiscalWatch’s five selected actions, and not additive to CBO policy estimates. GAO-26-108896 separately documents congressional matters and some prospective item-level figures; scopes, dates and budget horizons differ.

The GAO recommendation index links to its 2026 bulk CSV, with implementation statuses as of March 2026. The current status for each selected action should be rechecked on its own report’s Recommendations section. Public potential-benefit estimates are not available for many individual actions. Current-law adjustments, timing, net implementation costs, transfers and policy overlap must be reconciled before attributing new benefits. This is methodological guidance, not GAO endorsement or independent verification.

2. First five actions

Selection favors a mix of implementable oversight requests, quantified policy candidates and explicit evidence gaps. “Open” means the retrieved GAO record remains open; the dated comments may lag agency activity. This is a research shortlist, not five endorsed policy changes.

Medicare payments by place of service

GAO-16-189-M1 · Congress / HHS · Open – Partially Addressed.

Review remaining authority to equalize clinically comparable outpatient payments across settings. Request a current CBO score net of enacted laws and CMS rules, with rural access and beneficiary cost-sharing analysis.

Source estimate and period
$156.9 billion; 2025–2034; historical CBO broad alternative. Estimate source.
Costs and affected groups
Implementation, transition support, and any targeted hospital protections require a fresh score; not separately quantified here. Medicare beneficiaries, physician practices, hospitals, and rural or safety-net providers.
Trade-offs
Could reduce incentives to relocate routine services into higher-paid settings. Lower provider revenue could affect access; phase-in and service-level exceptions need evaluation.
Overlap and baseline
Do not add the broad CBO alternative to narrower imaging or drug-administration alternatives. Do not count already implemented policies again.
Published status notes
GAO comments through February 2026; includes November 2025 CMS rule. Retrieved 2026-10-22; this is the source’s status, not independent confirmation of implementation. GAO recommendation record.

Evaluate VA–DOD health care sharing

GAO-25-107497-R2 · Department of Veterans Affairs · Open.

Evaluate sharing agreements against performance goals and use findings to improve delivery. Request evaluation design, referral volumes, marginal costs and access metrics; assess a small facility pilot.

Source estimate and period
Tens of millions of dollars; Annually; qualitative potential in GAO 2026 annual report. Estimate source.
Costs and affected groups
Data collection, coordination and evaluation costs are unquantified; do not assume they are zero. Veterans, servicemembers, their families, and participating VA and DOD facilities.
Trade-offs
Sharing could improve access and utilization, but capacity, travel burden and continuity of care constrain expansion.
Overlap and baseline
Report net federal costs across both departments; interagency reimbursements alone are transfers, not savings.
Published status notes
Published status includes planned FY2027 Q3 performance review. Retrieved 2026-10-22; this is the source’s status, not independent confirmation of implementation. GAO recommendation record.

Evaluate nuclear waste classification options

GAO-26-108018-R1 · DOE Office of Environmental Management · Open.

Systematically assess lawful, safe treatment alternatives and communicate results and barriers to Congress. Obtain DOE evaluation evidence and independent technical review before endorsing any disposal change.

Source estimate and period
$73–210 billion; Lifecycle scenario in a 2020 DOE study cited by GAO; no annual profile. Estimate source.
Costs and affected groups
Lifecycle modeling, treatment facilities, transport, monitoring and litigation risks need scenario-level costing. Cleanup workers, nearby communities, Tribal nations, receiving jurisdictions and taxpayers.
Trade-offs
Potentially faster cleanup must satisfy safety and environmental requirements, consultation duties and long-term disposal standards.
Overlap and baseline
The scenario is not an incremental annual estimate; do not add it to other DOE cleanup totals.
Published status notes
August 2026 agency implementation assertion; GAO requests supporting evidence. Retrieved 2026-10-22; this is the source’s status, not independent confirmation of implementation. GAO recommendation record.

Improve Navy sustainment cost-risk analysis

GAO-20-2-R6 · Department of the Navy · Open – Partially Addressed.

Use risk and sensitivity analysis in shipbuilding operating and support cost estimates. Request evidence supporting GAO closure criteria and a program-level cost-risk comparison.

Source estimate and period
Billions of dollars; Potential across shipbuilding acquisition and sustainment; qualitative. Estimate source.
Costs and affected groups
Cost-estimating staff, engineering review and corrective actions are unquantified. Sailors, acquisition staff, shipyards, suppliers and defense taxpayers.
Trade-offs
Earlier risk analysis can improve choices; added review effort and delivery schedules must be balanced against readiness.
Overlap and baseline
The report’s $130 billion estimate growth is a cost problem, not a savings claim. Closed recommendations are not reopened here.
Published status notes
February 2026 comments: documentation of sensitivity analysis still needed. Retrieved 2026-10-22; this is the source’s status, not independent confirmation of implementation. GAO recommendation record.

Verify zero-income student loan applications

GAO-19-347-R1 · Education / Federal Student Aid · Open – Partially Addressed.

Confirm current verification controls and remaining gaps for applicants who self-certify zero income. Request post-September 2026 implementation status; establish current-law baseline before any new proposal.

Source estimate and period
More than $2 billion; 2020–2029 estimate associated with enacted FUTURE Act authority. Estimate source.
Costs and affected groups
Data matching, privacy safeguards, correction procedures and servicer implementation need costing. Student borrowers, loan servicers, Education and IRS data subjects.
Trade-offs
Verification could reduce errors but must accommodate income changes, protect tax data and allow corrections.
Overlap and baseline
Exclude the historical enacted-authority estimate from additional savings. Public award data cannot validate individual borrower eligibility.
Published status notes
December 2025 comments planned implementation by September 2026; completion needs reconfirmation. Retrieved 2026-10-22; this is the source’s status, not independent confirmation of implementation. GAO recommendation record.

3. National debt-reduction proposal

Ask Congress and agencies to review outstanding recommendations, commission current estimates, and implement only measures with defensible net benefits and access safeguards. Our initial policy priority is a fresh, service-specific assessment of site-neutral Medicare payments. Pair that with VA–DOD evaluation oversight and Navy cost-risk evidence requests. Keep nuclear-waste technical decisions and student-loan incremental estimates on hold until their distinct evidence gaps are resolved.

The following CBO comparisons preserve the original 2025–2034 window and assumed implementation dates. They are not scores for a new bill in October 2026. Current laws, rules, behavior and start dates need reconciliation. CBO presents options without endorsing them. CBO compendium.

Debt-service effects are not added. Legislative savings reduce borrowing relative to a baseline; they do not necessarily reduce nominal debt when the budget remains in deficit.

5. Reproducibility and uncertainty

Amounts are parsed as integer cents. Exact source-key repeats are removed; conflicting values under a key block analysis. CT keys include fiscal year, payment, invoice, line, distribution, account and fund. Native API responses are validated in TypeScript before caching in SQLite. Source refresh failures preserve the previous extract. Published extracts record exact queries, retrieval times, limitations, and response hashes.

7. Accountability standard

Track source confirmation, independent validation, submission receipt, agency response, adoption and realized savings separately. Require a counterfactual, implementation costs, time profile and overlap reconciliation for net savings. Disclose reviewer conflicts and publish dated corrections. Scaling to another country requires new legal, fiscal-year, currency and data contracts; no international connector is implemented.

Credible allegations can be referred through GAO FraudNet. No allegation is made or filed by this pilot. Treasury accepts voluntary gifts to reduce debt held by the public; personal donations are not required or proposed as the initiative’s funding method.
