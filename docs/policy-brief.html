Request: designate a staff contact to review a narrow evidence package, request updated agency status and obtain current fiscal analysis before endorsing implementation.

Why this merits review

GAO’s 2026 duplication report identifies hundreds of outstanding actions and substantial prospective benefits. Magnexis is building an open-source desktop research workspace to make selected actions easier to examine and follow through. We are not asserting new audit authority or independently verified savings. GAO annual report.

Three concrete requests

  1. Congress / HHS: request a current score and access assessment for remaining site-neutral Medicare payment reforms. The historical CBO broad alternative is $156.9 billion over 2025–2034; subsequent CMS changes and elapsed start dates mean it is not a current incremental estimate. CBO option; GAO status.
  2. VA / DOD: request the sharing-agreement evaluation protocol, costs and performance measures, including access and net federal cost across both departments. GAO recommendation 2.
  3. Navy: request documentation demonstrating operating/support cost sensitivity analysis and the remaining steps needed to meet GAO’s closure criteria. GAO recommendation 6.

Additional research, held for clarification

DOE waste-classification scenarios need independent technical and environmental review; student-loan verification needs post-September 2026 status and an enacted-law baseline. Neither is counted as new annual savings. The attached report documents all five actions and compares both spending and revenue policy options.

Connecticut pilot offer

We seek one willing state agency or audit liaison to clarify ledger definitions, select a document-backed procurement sample and assess a specific control improvement. The public-data extract establishes neither waste nor fraud. We welcome corrections before any allegation or savings claim.

Requested response

Please identify the appropriate budget, health, veterans, defense or oversight staff contact, indicate which request is within the office’s remit, and advise what evidence would make a follow-up useful. A 20-minute staff discussion would help narrow the research. We ask for review and feedback, not endorsement.
