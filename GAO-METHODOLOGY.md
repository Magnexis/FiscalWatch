# GAO 2026 methodology and source update

**Updated October 26, 2026.** This document incorporates public GAO research sources into the Magnexis FiscalWatch debt-reduction research pilot. The project is independent of GAO and does not claim GAO endorsement, verified fiscal savings, or adoption.

## New primary sources

- [GAO-26-108932 — Open Recommendations: Financial Benefits Could Be Between $132 Billion and $251 Billion](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108932). Published May 12, 2026. This is a modeled *future* potential-benefit range across GAO's broad open-recommendations inventory, based on an October 2025 observation. It is **not annual savings**, not realized savings, not an estimate for FiscalWatch's selected five recommendations, and not a value to add to any existing option.
- [GAO-26-108896 — Recommendations for Congress](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108896). Published May 12, 2026. This separately examines open matters for Congress, including item-level potential financial benefits. Its scopes, estimation dates and horizons differ from the portfolio model.
- [2026 Duplication and Cost Savings Recommendations](https://www.gao.gov/duplication-cost-savings/duplication-cost-savings-recommendations), with the [direct 2026 CSV](https://www.gao.gov/assets/2026-05/2026%20Duplication%20and%20Cost%20Savings%20-%20Downloadable%20Spreadsheet.csv). The download contains **implementation statuses as of March 2026**, rather than live statuses. The CSV had returned HTTP 403 during FiscalWatch's initial acquisition. This link is now documented, but a successful downloaded snapshot has **not** yet been independently verified.

## Methodology and implementation-status protocol

1. Match each exact **GAO report and recommendation/matter ID**. Do not treat all actions in the same report as having the same status.
2. Open the **Recommendations** section of the individual GAO report, rather than relying solely on the March 2026 CSV. Record the displayed GAO status, status-comments date, URL, and actual retrieval date. Agency implementation claims are not necessarily GAO-verified closure.
3. Treat prospective GAO modeled benefits, selected item estimates, historical CBO options, implemented historical benefits, independently verified realized savings and research targets as **different accounting categories**.
4. Before presenting incremental net fiscal effects, account for enacted policy, applicable baseline, budget window, transition and implementation costs, transfers, timing, distributional effects, and program-specific constraints.
5. **Do not aggregate overlapping estimates.** Examples include alternative site-neutral Medicare payment policies; CBO and GAO estimates addressing similar measures; enacted savings already reflected in a baseline; interagency reimbursements that are merely transfers; and DOE lifecycle scenarios that are not annual savings.
6. If scope overlap, current law, or the cost baseline is unresolved, preserve separately labeled estimates without a total. Seek independent review and a new current-law score before claiming verified net savings.
7. Track source confirmation, agency response, implementation, independent verification, and fiscal realization separately. A report being open or partially addressed does not establish that its maximum prospective benefit is available.

## Current project status

The five-action FiscalWatch register remains a curated shortlist, not a complete GAO recommendation database. The **$100 million annual verification objective is a research target**; **independently verified annual savings remain $0**. The May 2026 GAO reports and dated CSV guide further research, but are not evidence of achieved debt reduction by Magnexis.

This methodology was updated after GAO staff provided links to these official public resources. The response was guidance, **not** GAO endorsement, a partnership agreement, adoption, or an audit of the project. Private correspondence is not reproduced here.
