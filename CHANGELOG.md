# Changelog

## Unreleased — 2026-10-26 GAO methodology refresh

- Incorporated GAO-26-108932 and GAO-26-108896 into desktop, README and published research sources.
- Linked the official 2026 Duplication and Cost Savings CSV, while distinguishing the March 2026 bulk snapshot from live report-page statuses.
- Documented modeled potential benefits and no-overlap rules without reporting them as realized or annual FiscalWatch savings.
- Added an opt-in local CSV downloader with hash, redirect and shape checks, plus offline tests; no unverified GAO affiliation or download claim.


## 1.3.0 — Government decision workspace

- Added the Fiscal Strategy board: a colorful, assumption-linked debt trajectory chart and a sequenced research agenda for stress testing, evidence review, and disclosure.
- Added a pure, tested strategy derivation that categorizes only the *illustrative scenario's* debt-ratio direction. It does not score governments, forecast outcomes, or claim a policy will reduce debt.
- Made the 1.3.0 release identifier consistent across npm, Cargo, and Tauri package metadata.
- Expanded release documentation, repository metadata guidance, and change history for a publish-ready project surface.
- Switched the Windows HTTP client to native SChannel TLS, removing the toolchain-specific `ring` archive failure that blocked the portable desktop build.

## 0.3.0 — Fiscal Pulse release

- Reframed the desktop home surface as a colorful, guided Fiscal Pulse with explicit next actions for fiscal position, spending records, and opportunity evidence.
- Added deterministic research-readiness logic and regression coverage. It never produces a government score, savings estimate, fraud signal, or performance rating.
- Added the official original Fiscal Watch SVG mark and integrated it into the desktop shell and README.
- Raised package and Tauri release metadata to 0.3.0 while retaining local-first persistence and source-boundary controls.

## 0.2.0 — Phase 2 foundation

- Added the fiscal intelligence workspace, U.S. country profile, native Treasury debt refresh, and deterministic debt-scenario export.
- Added local opportunity registry records and hashed local audit entries.
- Added source, scenario, registry, and native validation coverage plus a Windows verification workflow.
- Added a threat model and dependency inventory.
- Kept non-retrieved fiscal series unavailable instead of substituting synthetic or incompatible data.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-22

- Initial Windows desktop research pilot with Tauri, React, TypeScript, Rust and SQLite.
- Five source-linked GAO actions, explicitly separated from independently verified benefits.
- CT payment and USAspending contract/grant extracts, official refresh and validated imports.
- Cent-exact summaries, protected identity handling, conflict detection and invoice review candidates.
- Historical CBO policy comparison with overlapping-alternative checks.
- Local follow-up notes, evidence links, backup/restore and export.
- Printable fiscal report, congressional brief and static GitHub Pages companion.

Verified annual savings remain $0. Independent review, invoice reconciliation, legislative scoring and government adoption are not complete.
